LAMINA / LOCAL APP

Turn source files into work you can use.

Set a goal, add sources, and choose a document, guide, assessment, or listening script. Lamina plans the work, coordinates reading and writing, shows source passages alongside the result, and lets you revise a section.

Download LaminaPython + local app · Your model adapter · MIT license
HOW A PROJECT RUNS

From source files to a reviewed result.

Lamina divides the reading, plans for the goal, and gives each writer the source evidence and prerequisite ideas it needs. Review findings stay with the result. You can inspect the output and request a revision to one section. A configured audio adapter can turn a listening script into a WAV file.

01Set the goal

Name the audience and outcome. Add text, Markdown, or PDF sources.

02Read and plan

Readers work on separate passages with neighboring context. Their findings inform a shared plan.

03Write and review

Writers follow the plan. Review records findings against the result.

04Open and revise

Read the output with its source evidence. Revise a section when it needs work.
