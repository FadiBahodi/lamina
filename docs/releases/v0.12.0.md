Lamina 0.12 restores the two-call-deep sweep as a first-class route, removes the barriers from the planned route, and plans podcasts in episodes.

## Changes

- **Flashcard decks (`--format cards`).** Every reader window is one independent call that writes cards with span citations. Duplicates are suppressed locally in two lanes: identical text, or similarity above a threshold (provider embeddings when the adapter offers `embed`, otherwise TF-IDF) with agreement on numbers, negations, abbreviations and word order; a pair that differs in one of those is kept and listed as related. Each card is compared against the kept set only, so chains of distinct claims cannot collapse. A deterministic sample of windows gets one source-centred audit call whose omission and unsupported findings are attached, never gating. A deck is `ready` when every window was read and no finding is open, otherwise `review` with the gaps listed. Exports an Anki text file with note types and stable GUIDs, and `cards.json` with every citation.
- **Reading at depth one.** Every reader also sees the last six sentence spans of the preceding unit and the first six of the following unit, with their real span IDs, so a boundary cut is visible without a second serial call. The validator indexes exactly those spans, so a citation of a neighbouring span the request never showed is rejected. The halo shrinks to fit the reader budget. Failed reads are recorded and the run continues by default.
- **Calibration promotes demonstrated load.** A passing ceiling counts only up to the largest request that actually ran at that stage.
- **Planning without barriers.** Grouping starts on the pages already read while other reads are still running; a grouping level starts as soon as a full batch of summaries exists and only once those summaries already exceed the outline budget. Level-0 batches keep source order; the outline sees one original card per group. Only the outline call and the assignment batch remain as barriers.
- **Podcasts in episodes.** The outline numbers sections into episodes with spoken-word targets sized from `--episode-minutes`; `--episodes N` is enforced. Each section is synthesized the moment it leaves review on a speech lane bounded by the adapter's concurrency, and delivery assembles one WAV per episode beside the full programme. Scripts still in review render by default.
- **Bounded comparison.** `compare_relations` sends only similarity-nominated pairs above a threshold (plus same-unit and same-structure lanes) to a model call.
- **Defaults.** `workers` and the Gemini adapter's `max_concurrency` are 16. The Gemini adapter accepts a per-stage thinking map and reports admission wait separately from transport time.
- **Receipts measure their own schedule.** Every tracked call records its start, end and declared predecessors; each plan and receipt reports the critical path, total model time, the bound `max(W/P, D)`, the measured wall and their ratio, calls in flight and serial time. Exported documents end with the run's timeline. `lamina demo --synthetic-latency` produces one from the fixture, labelled synthetic.
- **Invariants as property tests.** Spans partition a text exactly; a reader can cite exactly the halo spans it was shown; every idea has one owner or an explicit omission; identical cards collapse and cards that differ in a number, negation or abbreviation never merge; the schedule bound never exceeds the measured span.
- **App.** The home page offers the flashcard setup; results show the deck, audit findings, Anki download and per-episode scripts. The app is one page: projects, project setups and the method editor.

## Removed

- The fixed-format document builder from the 0.2 to 0.9 releases: its lesson pipeline, procedure presets, short-answer and oral-case outputs, the `web/` reader and the `build`, `run`, `export`, `validate` and `serve` commands. `lamina demo` now runs the production engine on a bundled fixture. Source material ingested with earlier releases is unaffected; only the generated bundles belonged to the old builder.
- The hosted demo, the recorded in-app example and the `lamina studio` alias (use `lamina app`).
- Historic benchmark dumps from earlier protocol revisions and screenshots of removed screens. Documentation is consolidated into fourteen guides plus the paper.

## Why

[Flow geometry](https://github.com/FadiBahodi/lamina/blob/main/docs/flow-geometry.md) models the routes as dependency graphs: the longest serial chain on each route, the work that waits for a full batch, the cost of the boundary halo and of grouped requests, with the assumptions behind each calculation. The sweep's chain is two calls: read, then audit. A planned route with `L` grouping levels has a chain of `5 + L` calls (read, `L` grouping calls, outline, assignment, write, review) before repairs, context extensions or subdivision lengthen individual paths; streaming lets independent branches of the grouping tree overlap.

## Validation and installation

Validation for the tagged revision is recorded in the repository's CI.

```sh
pip install 'lamina_engine-0.12.0-py3-none-any.whl[pdf,slides,http,tokens]'
lamina app
```

Private source documents, model responses and credentials are excluded from the release.
