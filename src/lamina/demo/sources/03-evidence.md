# Resilient workflow engineering

## Evidence makes a release reviewable

An evidence receipt links a published claim to the exact source unit, the operation that transformed it, and the artifact users received. A count of processed pages proves only that pages were visited; it does not prove that every important idea was extracted or taught. Keep assigned, deferred, and unexamined material distinct.

Review the authored lesson and the delivered medium separately. A correct script can become inaudible or truncated in audio; clean transcription cannot establish that an explanation teaches well. A release claim should name what was checked, what a human can inspect, and what remains uncertain. Use the receipt to reopen the smallest failed stage instead of rerunning everything.
