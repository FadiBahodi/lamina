The queue changed the experience.

A saved batch of 19 course jobs spent much more time waiting than running. In one part, planning took 73.754 seconds; the slowest of eight writers took 4.122 seconds. That called for attention to admission, planning and the media queue.

Read the measurement context

LOWER BOUND ON COMPLETION TIMET ≥ max(D, maxr Wr / cr)D is the longest dependency chain. Wr is the work assigned to resource r; cr is its capacity. Queueing, retries and other overhead add to this bound.
