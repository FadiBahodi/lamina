HOW IT WORKS

Use the structure the work needs.

Automatic mode starts with the simplest path that fits the input. Every section is checked against its sources.

Write directly

For a small collection, the writer receives your sources and goal. A reviewer checks the result. There is no separate extraction or planning call.

Plan sections

For larger collections, readers extract cited ideas and a planner assigns them to sections. Writers receive their own evidence and a short shared outline.

Supply assignments

If you or another agent already know the structure, supply the sections and source references through Python or the CLI. Lamina handles writing, review and export.

One call limit controls concurrency. A finished section can be reviewed while others are being written. Revisions reuse unchanged requests. See the measurements and their limits.
