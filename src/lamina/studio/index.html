LAMINA / LOCAL PRODUCTION SOFTWARE

Turn a source collection into a finished piece of work.

Describe the goal, add your material, and choose how much work may run at once. Lamina organizes the reading, coordinates writing and review, keeps a route back to the source, and lets you revise the result.

THE PRODUCT

You choose the job. The system handles the handoffs.

A guide, assessment, or listening script needs different editorial decisions. Lamina reads your sources in bounded pieces, lets the model shape a plan for the goal, and carries relevant source and prior work to each writer. The app delivers text and documents; a configured audio adapter can also render a listening script as a WAV file.

03Write + review

Writers build the artifact and a review records what passed or needs attention.

04Open + revise

Inspect the result and its source evidence. Revisit a gap and rerun affected work.

CONTEXT IS A DESIGN CHOICE

A reader gets the passage it owns and the source units beside it.

PREVIOUS / neighboring source unitOWNED SOURCE PASSAGENEXT / neighboring source unit

Neighbors help interpret a boundary. A writer receives the shared plan, its assigned ideas, and exact evidence needed from prerequisite sections. It does not receive another writer's completed prose. More context has a cost; less context can break the connection between ideas.
